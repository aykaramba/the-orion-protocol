# The Orion Protocol

## Project Overview

A classified deep-space communications network designed to maintain contact
with exploration vessels beyond the Kuiper Belt. The protocol operates on
a rotating cipher system synchronized to pulsar timing signatures.

## System Components

- **Beacon Array**: Ground-based transmission nodes located at Stations Alpha, Beta, and Gamma
- **Relay Satellites**: Mid-range signal boosters in geostationary orbit
- **Deep Field Receivers**: Ship-mounted antenna clusters tuned to sub-light frequencies
