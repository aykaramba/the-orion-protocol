# Chapter 4: Beyond the Horizon

The source of the mirrored signal revealed itself at 0347 UTC. A structure
— vast, dark, and geometrically precise — emerged from the sensor noise.
It had been there the entire time, hidden in the absorption field's blind
spot.

## Structure Profile

- Classification: unknown artificial construct
- Dimensions: approximately 4.1 km along longest axis
- Surface temperature: 2.7 K — indistinguishable from background radiation
- Signal output: continuous, matching Orion Protocol headers exactly

## Voss's Final Log

> "It learned our language before we even knew it was listening. Every
> transmission we sent through the protocol — it absorbed, decoded, and
> reconstructed. The Orion Protocol wasn't just reaching into the dark.
> Something in the dark was reaching back."

## Project Status

- Orion Protocol: compromised — all cipher layers breached by unknown entity
- Vessel Artemis: holding position, 0.4 AU from structure
- Ground contact: severed — last outbound signal rejected by absorption field
- Recommendation: await further contact — we are no longer alone
