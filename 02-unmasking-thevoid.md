# Chapter 2: Unmasking the Void

Dr. Kaplan isolated the source of the signal decay. It wasn't equipment
failure — something was actively absorbing transmissions in the 4.4 GHz
band. The interference pattern was too regular to be natural.

## Interference Profile

- Pattern type: periodic nulling, 11.2-second cycle
- Absorption bandwidth: 4.38–4.54 GHz
- Origin bearing: 14.7° offset from Artemis heading
- No catalogued objects at that bearing

## Kaplan's Analysis

> "This isn't scattering or attenuation. Something is catching our
> signals and not letting them through. The periodicity suggests
> rotation — like a lighthouse in reverse. Instead of broadcasting,
> it's silencing."

## Revised Status

- Beacon Array: recalibrated, no improvement
- Cipher rotation: deployed, signals still absorbed
- New directive: investigate interference source
