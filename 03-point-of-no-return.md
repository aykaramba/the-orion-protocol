# Chapter 3: Point of No Return

Artemis crossed the threshold. The absorption field wasn't a barrier — it
was a boundary marker. Beyond it, the Orion Protocol's encryption layer
began rewriting itself without ground authorization.

## System Anomalies

- Cipher rotation: now autonomous, cycling every 3.7 seconds
- Beacon handshake: rejected by all three ground stations
- Navigation lock: Maren reports star charts recalibrating to unknown reference frame

## Tanaka's Field Report

> "The antenna array is receiving something, but it's not from Earth.
> The signal structure matches our own protocol — same headers, same
> timing signatures — but the content is incomprehensible. It's like
> hearing your own voice speaking a language you've never learned."

## Mission Decision

Commander Voss has ordered Artemis to continue forward. Returning to
charted space would require passing through the absorption field again,
and Kaplan believes the protocol modifications may not survive the
transition back.

There is no turning around.
