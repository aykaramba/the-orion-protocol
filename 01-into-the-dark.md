# Chapter 1: Into the Dark

The last transmission from Vessel Artemis arrived fragmented. Commander Voss
reported anomalous signal decay beyond Relay Station Gamma — the kind of
degradation that shouldn't occur within the protocol's operational envelope.

## Signal Analysis

- Carrier frequency: 4.462 GHz (nominal)
- Signal-to-noise ratio: dropped from 42 dB to 11 dB over 72 hours
- Pulsar sync offset: 0.003 ms — within tolerance but drifting

## Voss's Log Entry

> "We're still transmitting, but I'm not confident anyone is receiving.
> The array feels like it's pointing into nothing. Kaplan is running
> diagnostics. Maren says the nav data looks fine, but the stars out
> here don't match the charts."
